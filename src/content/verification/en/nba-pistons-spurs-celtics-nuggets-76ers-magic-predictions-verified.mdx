## Pistons 105-93 Spurs: the Paint Wasn't Overrun, and the Spurs' Shooting Ended the Streak

Before the game I wrote that the Spurs extending the streak to 13 was "close to a given" and that they had about a 70% chance of winning by 10 or more; with the margin removed the market had the Spurs at 77.1%, so **I was more confident than the market**. The Spurs lost by 12 and the 12-game streak is over. San Antonio led 33-26 after the first quarter, trailed 55-54 at halftime and were outscored 50-39 in the second half.

**Half wrong: "Without Duren the Pistons' paint will be overrun by Wembanyama."** Rebounds were 43 to 43 (offensive rebounds 13 to 15), and Detroit won points in the paint 60 to 44 (ESPN). Wembanyama had 25 points and 18 rebounds, but he was 11 of 23, 1 of 8 from three and had 3 turnovers; he did have a clear edge inside, but he made just 1 of 8 threes and his teammates were just as cold from the perimeter, which let the Pistons shrink the paint and make up for their disadvantage inside.

**Wrong: "With Robinson out, Detroit's only perimeter shooter is Huerter."** Huerter was 0 of 3 from three (and had 4 turnovers); the Pistons shot 6 of 25 (24.0%) as a team, but won on 60 points in the paint, 24 fast-break points (ESPN) and 25 from Jenkins (11 of 21). They didn't win it from the outside.

**Wrong: "Cunningham alone can't hold off three guards plus Wembanyama."** Cunningham had 27 points and was +16, and his teammates were excellent: Jenkins 25, Thompson 14 points, 9 rebounds, 12 assists and 5 steals, and Reed 10 points in just 24:44 at +20. He didn't just hold up, he lifted his teammates too.

**Half right: the three-guard lineup "has the edge in rotation speed."** Castle, Fox and Harper combined for 14 of 35 (40.0%) and 8 turnovers, with Fox on 6 points (-11) and Castle 3 of 11; Harper's 20 points were the bright spot. The speed edge was real, but their poor perimeter shooting is what gave San Antonio this loss.

**Right (result right, reason wrong): the under at 223.** The total was 198, 25 under the line, but not for the reason I gave ("Detroit's offense drops without Robinson and San Antonio gives its bench early minutes"): both teams were cold from the perimeter, combining for 15 of 65 from three (23.1%), and the Spurs' starters all played 31 to 36 minutes, with no early rest.

**Right: "neither team's results are high quality."** Before the game I noted that 6 of the Spurs' 12 wins came against teams at 4-9 or worse and that the Pistons were only 3-4 against teams over .500; in this head-to-head, the Pistons won.

## 76ers 121-116 Magic: Without Maxey, Philadelphia Won on Free Throws and the Fourth Quarter

The 76ers moneyline and -2 both landed (won by 5), and the under missed (total 237, 15 over the line). Orlando led 40-27 after the first quarter and 69-64 at halftime, the third quarter was 28-28, and the 76ers turned it around with a 29-19 fourth quarter; the Magic's largest lead was 17 (ESPN).

**Right: 76ers moneyline and -2.** They won by 5 and took the fourth quarter 29-19, but not for the reasons I wrote.

**Not there before the game, there after: Maxey's absence.** The pre-game article named Maxey (26.5 points a game) as the first scoring option and wrote about his matchup against Black and Suggs; he didn't play (ESPN: right foot), and neither did Wade (ESPN: right hamstring), and neither was on the 11/17 injury page I had compiled. The 76ers got their 121 points from Edgecombe (24 in 43:15), Embiid 26, LeBron 22 and Simons 18, with Philon Jr. adding 10. The conclusion of "even without Brown they still have three scoring threats" held, but the names changed by one; the pre-game piece only said to check the official injury report on whether LeBron and Embiid would play, and didn't mention Maxey.

**Right: Embiid playing.** He played 36:13 for 26 points, but was 7 of 18 from the field and 0 of 1 from three, and the scoring came from a 12 of 14 night at the line; the "Embiid shoots 40%" weakness noted before the game showed up again (38.9%).

**Wrong: "Orlando's three-point percentage is near the bottom of the league, so there is little firepower."** Orlando was on fire from three, shooting over 50%, 19 of 37 (51.4%), with Suggs 5 of 6 and Bane 5 of 9. The Magic actually scored more points from three than the 76ers (57 to 42), but in the key moments they lost at the line (9 of 17 against 23 of 26, so the 76ers had 14 more points from the line) and lacked a way to break the defense down, and their 19 points in the fourth quarter was the biggest reason they lost.

**Right: "Wagner lacks steady shooting and mostly attacks the rim."** Wagner had 27 points and 11 assists on 1 of 6 from three; he really did score by attacking the rim (12 of 22) and passing, and although his efficiency was good, when the defense collapsed late he still lacked a steady way to break it down.

**Wrong: "Orlando's turnovers (15.8 a game) are the most of the four teams."** Orlando had only 11 turnovers in this game, fewer than the 76ers' 12.

**Wrong: the under at 222.** The total was 237, and the two teams combined to shoot 86 of 165 (52.1%); both sides shot very well, and the game looked nothing like two sides that are strong on defense and weak on offense.

## Nuggets 105-101 Celtics: the Three-Point Risk I Named Before the Game Came True

The Celtics moneyline, -2 and over all missed. Boston led 30-24 after the first quarter and 61-49 at halftime (the largest lead was 17, ESPN), were outscored 31-19 in the third quarter to tie it at 80-80, and lost the second half 56-40; Denver's largest lead was only 4 (ESPN).

**Wrong: Celtics moneyline and -2.** They lost by 4, led by 12 at halftime and were outscored 56-40 in the second half.

**Right (the risk came true): "the weakness of the Celtics' moneyball style is that when everyone's shot is off, they can lose to anyone."** The biggest risk I named before the game was that Boston can't keep up steady output when their three-point shooting is cold, and in this game they went 11 of 44 (25.0%), with White 0 of 7, George 1 of 7 and Jayson Tatum 3 of 8. Compared with their season numbers of 43.9 attempts and 38.2%, they took enough shots and just didn't make them. But taking on risk is part of sports betting, and nobody could have known before the game how their shooting would be that night.

**Half right, half wrong: "the Nuggets' defense has declined, allowing 113.2."** The Celtics scored only 101 (against a season average of 114.8), but that was mostly down to their own cold shooting rather than anything clever about Denver's defensive scheme. The Nuggets shot just 7 of 31 from three (22.6%) themselves, and they won the game at the free-throw line (24 of 29).

**Wrong: "George has bounced back since changing teams and shows none of the old slow-footed veteran on defense."** In this game George played only 22:31, shot 3 of 12 and fouled out with 6 fouls. One game doesn't overturn a whole season, but that line didn't hold up here.

**Half right: "Aaron Gordon is in poor form and can't deliver steady defense."** Gordon had 13 points and 8 rebounds (5 of 10) and was +7; he wasn't bad in this game, and one game doesn't prove anything either way.

**Wrong: the over at 222.** The total was 206, 16 under the line; the two teams combined for 18 of 75 from three (24.0%) and 77 of 183 overall (42.1%), so my claim that both teams are top five in offensive efficiency didn't hold on a night when neither could hit a three.

---

This is the age of the three-pointer, and also an age of big betting risk. Two of this week's predictions missed, and it wasn't only me: the market's lines missed too. The biggest reason for both misses was the three-point shot. Nobody before the game should have expected the Spurs to lose to a Pistons team missing key players, and on the Celtics I did say that if their threes didn't fall they would likely lose, but I didn't expect them to shoot quite this badly. In a modern game built around the three, a hot or cold hand on either side can completely upend a pre-game read of who is stronger, and even in the game I did call right, the 76ers were nearly toppled by the Magic's unexpected accuracy. Betting carries risk, so please bet responsibly.
