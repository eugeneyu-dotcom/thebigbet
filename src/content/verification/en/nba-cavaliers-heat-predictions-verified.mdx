## Heat 133-121 Cavaliers: I Saw the Weakness but Bet on the Wrong Thing

**Game flow**: the quarter scores were Heat 29-25-38-41 and Cavaliers 27-30-34-30. Cleveland led 57-54 at halftime, Miami went up 92-91 after three quarters, and the Heat won the fourth 41-30 to pull away. There were 12 lead changes; Cleveland's largest lead was only 6 and Miami's 13, and the Heat led for 69% of the game against Cleveland's 23% (ESPN numbers).

**Injuries and starters**: Cleveland were without Mobley (toe sprain) and Merrill (right elbow), and started Tyson, Watson, Allen, Harden and Donovan Mitchell. On the Heat side, **Larsson, who was questionable before the game, does not appear in the official box score** (ESPN lists him as out with illness), and **Thompson is listed as DNP (coach's decision)**; ESPN gives no reason, so ⚠️ I couldn't confirm why he sat. Giannis, probable beforehand, played. Miami started Giannis, Gardner, Adebayo, Hardaway Jr. and Davion Mitchell, with Wiggins and Conwell off the bench. The scenario I wrote before the game, "if Thompson and Larsson are both out, it's down to Wiggins, Hardaway Jr. and Conwell", did happen, but not with the result I expected; see the review below.

**Wrong: "The Heat's inconsistency; Adebayo and Thompson have repeatedly shot under 20% in a game."** This was my third reason for backing Cleveland, and it was the most completely wrong. [Giannis](wiki:Giannis_Antetokounmpo) went 15/20 in just 31:00 for 37 points and 9 rebounds; [Adebayo](wiki:Bam_Adebayo) was 13/19 and 9/10 at the line in 38:05 for 37 points and 14 rebounds (4 offensive); and off the bench Conwell had 20 points in 32:45 (4/9 from three). Miami shot 46/88 (52.3%) as a team, had 37 assists and only 9 turnovers. I used "they've been very inconsistent" as a reason, but that is a season-long sample, not a prediction for this game.

**Wrong: "Cleveland still have Allen to limit Giannis, and Adebayo will struggle to punish a paint without Mobley."** [Allen](wiki:Jarrett_Allen) had only 9 points and 8 rebounds in 31:40 and committed 3 fouls, and Giannis was 15/20 without being contained; the Heat won points in the paint 56-40, offensive rebounds 13-7 and free-throw attempts 37-25. Adebayo scored 37 in a paint without Mobley, the exact opposite of my read.

**Half right: "Miami's three-point defense will be shot up by Cleveland."** The Cavaliers went 18 of 47 from three (38.3%), above their season average of about 41 attempts and above the 16.1 threes (an estimate) the Heat allow per game, so the weakness is real. But I put the emphasis on [Donovan Mitchell](wiki:Donovan_Mitchell) and [Harden](wiki:James_Harden) as the pull-up shooters, and the pair went only 7/20 (35%) from three combined, with Mitchell 5/12 and Harden 2/8; the threes came mainly from Proctor (5/7), Tyson (4/7) and Meleek Thomas (2/5). More to the point, Cleveland scored 54 points from three against Miami's 36, and the Heat made it up and then some with 56 points in the paint and a 29/37 night at the line.

**Half right: "Use Harden properly and the Cavaliers' offense can improve."** The offense really wasn't the problem: 121 points, 48.8% shooting and only 9 turnovers (below their season average of about 13.5). Harden played 34:49, took 13 shots, went 8/8 at the line and finished with 20 points, 8 assists and 2 turnovers, and his role looked the same as in the previous game. But his own efficiency wasn't good (5/13, 2/8 from three), and Cleveland's problem was never on the offensive end; it is the next point.

**Right: "Cleveland's weakness is rim protection and rebounding without Mobley."** I wrote this in the pre-game "specific tactical weaknesses" section: rebounds were 36 for Cleveland and 46 for Miami, points in the paint 40-56, fouls 29 to 20, and the Heat attempted 12 more free throws. That is exactly where the game was decided, but none of my three reasons for backing Cleveland counted it, so I weighted the conclusion wrongly.

**Wrong: "A slight lean to the under."** The total was 254, 18.5 above the 235.5 line, and losing Mobley didn't drag the score down at all. I estimate about 100 possessions (a box-score formula estimate, not the official Pace), which isn't fast; the total blew up because both teams had effective field-goal percentages near 59% (about 59.3% for Cleveland and 59.1% for Miami), not because of tempo.

**Right (for reference): rest, depth and the market.** Miami had one more day of rest, which was a small edge; on depth I wrote "Cleveland have a slight edge", and bench points were 47 for Cleveland and 33 for Miami, which looks right but had no effect on the result. On the market, 16 of 30 books had the Heat at -2.5 and about 55.2% after removing the margin; the Heat not only won but won by 12, so the market's read was better than my contrarian one.

## Summary: I Picked the Right Weakness but the Wrong Players to Exploit It

The most interesting thing about this game is that both of the structural problems I flagged before tip-off were real. Miami did allow 18 threes, and Cleveland without Mobley were beaten on the boards and in the paint. But I put my case for Cleveland on Harden's role, on Mitchell's and Harden's threes, and on the Heat being "inconsistent", and Miami's two bigs scoring 37 apiece knocked all of that over at once. Of my three reasons for Cleveland, one was wrong and two were only half right; the one that was entirely right, "rim protection and rebounding without Mobley", sat in the analysis section and never reached the conclusion, and it points to Miami's side.

I said before the game that "unless Thompson turns in another miracle performance, the edge is with Cleveland." Thompson didn't play at all and Miami still won by 12; with two questionable players, Thompson and Larsson, both out, Gardner, Conwell and Davion Mitchell (22 points and 17 assists) covered the gap. That is the thing I underrated in the pre-game piece: the absences were asymmetric. Cleveland were missing rim protection and Miami were missing perimeter rotation players, and Miami's hole was easier to fill. That is also why betting always carries risk; this verification is for reference only, so please bet responsibly.
