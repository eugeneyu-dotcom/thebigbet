## Knicks 111-97 Pistons: The Reasons Mostly Held, and Towns Dominated a Frontcourt Without Duren

**Knicks 111-97 Pistons.** Beforehand we said the Knicks ([the defending champions](/en/nba/trends/nba-atlantic-division-2026-27-preview/)) still had their defensive base and system and would win as long as the perimeter shots fell, and that the Pistons missing [Duren](wiki:Jalen_Duren) would leave questions inside (see the [Central Division preview](/en/nba/trends/nba-central-division-2026-27-preview/) on the Pistons' frontcourt). Both points were basically right: [Bridges](wiki:Mikal_Bridges) finally opened his three-point account for the season (4/8 from three, 12 points, ending his 0-for-10 start), and the Knicks played with relative ease. [Towns](wiki:Karl-Anthony_Towns) pulled down 20 rebounds (27 points on 12/21), 8 of them offensive, so the Pistons' frontcourt really was overwhelmed by him. [Brunson](wiki:Jalen_Brunson) shot only 5/18 but went 13/16 from the line to hold up his scoring, the Knicks as a team shot 23/28 from the line against the Pistons' 14/24, and off the bench [Shamet](wiki:Landry_Shamet) scored 13 (3/5 from three) and [Drummond](wiki:Andre_Drummond) added 10 in only 14 minutes (plus-minus +16). For the Pistons, starting centre [Reed](wiki:Paul_Reed_(basketball)) scored 22 (9/12), but [Robinson](wiki:Duncan_Robinson) shot 2/10 from three, the team shot 9/37 (24.3%) from three, [Collins](wiki:John_Collins_(basketball)) had only 2 points, and [Cunningham](wiki:Cade_Cunningham) had 19 points and 8 assists on 7/18 shooting. The fallback "under 219.5" also landed with a total of just 208, but that was because the two teams combined to make only 21 of 73 threes.

## Trail Blazers 136-129 Lakers: The Main Pick Hit, but "Dončić's Stamina" Never Happened

**Trail Blazers 136-129 Lakers.** Beforehand I thought **[Dončić](wiki:Luka_Dončić), on only one day of rest and with his stamina down, might not be able to carry the team alone**, but he still scored 41 with 9 assists in 36:45, as strong as ever, so the stamina reason didn't hold. What actually made my prediction right were the other two reasons I mentioned: the Blazers being the more complete team, and the Lakers' poor backcourt defence (see the [Northwest Division preview](/en/nba/trends/nba-northwest-division-2026-27-preview/) and the [Pacific Division preview](/en/nba/trends/nba-pacific-division-2026-27-preview/)). Against the Lakers' defence, the Blazers shot **59% from the field and 50% from three (18/36)**, almost helping themselves from the perimeter, and the Lakers giving up 136 shows why their defensive rating is only 19th in the league is no accident; the over landed easily (265, 31 above the line). [Avdija](wiki:Deni_Avdija) had 23 points and 9 assists and did make use of his matchup edge, with [Morant](wiki:Ja_Morant) adding 23 (9/13), [Holiday](wiki:Jrue_Holiday) 22 (8/12), [Lillard](wiki:Damian_Lillard) 21 (4/8 from three) and [Robert Williams III](wiki:Robert_Williams_III) 14, while reserve [Henderson](wiki:Scoot_Henderson) had only 2 points and 5 turnovers, which didn't matter. For the Lakers, [Reaves](wiki:Austin_Reaves) played the full 39:15 for 26 points, reserve [Mamukelashvili](wiki:Sandro_Mamukelashvili) scored 17, and [Kessler](wiki:Walker_Kessler) had 13 points and 8 rebounds but 4 turnovers.

## Summary: Two Hits, but for Different Reasons

In the Knicks game I did foresee Bridges' shooting recovering and Towns having a big edge against a Pistons frontcourt without Duren, so both the call and the reasons were right. In the Lakers game the call was right, but the main reason was not Dončić's declining stamina; it was the defence and roster-completeness points I raised on the side, and neither of those was what I considered the main reason before the game, just something I mentioned in passing, so I can't say my reasoning really landed.
