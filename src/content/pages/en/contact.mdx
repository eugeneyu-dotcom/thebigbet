## What you can write to us about

- **Corrections**: if you spot a wrong score, player detail, date or statistic in an article, send the page URL and what you believe is correct, and once we confirm it we will fix it directly.
- **Problem reports**: pages that display incorrectly, broken links, things that don't work on mobile, and so on.
- **Privacy**: questions about the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy/), or how your data is handled.
- **Partnership enquiries**: collaboration on content, data or technology.

## One thing to note

The site is run by a small anonymous team and **does not offer any betting services**. If you have an account question about betting, please contact the betting platform you use directly.

To learn how we make our content, see our [Editorial Policy](/en/editorial-policy/); to learn about our team, see [About](/en/about/).
