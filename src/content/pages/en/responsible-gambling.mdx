This site offers match analysis and odds information. It **does not encourage or facilitate gambling**. If you decide to bet, treat it as entertainment with a cost, not as a way to make money.

## Age and the law

- You must not take part in any gambling if you are under 18 (or the legal age where you live).
- Gambling is legal in some places and not others. Check the law where you are, and only use operators licensed locally.
- This site offers no betting service and does not endorse any betting operator.

## Basic principles for staying in control

1. **Set a budget first**: use only money you can afford to lose — never rent, living expenses or borrowed money.
2. **Set limits and stick to them**: decide in advance how much and how long you'll bet each time and each week, and stop when you reach it.
3. **Don't chase losses**: raising your stakes to win back what you lost is the most common and most dangerous habit.
4. **Don't treat a prediction as a guarantee**: even the most thorough analysis is only probability, and even the games we like most can go wrong — which is why we openly review our misses.
5. **Keep the rest of your life going**: betting shouldn't take over your work, family or sleep.

## Warning signs

If any of these apply to you, seriously consider pausing and getting help:

- Your stakes or how often you bet keep growing, and you find it hard to stop.
- You lie or hide things because of gambling, or borrow money for it.
- After losing you feel low or irritable and can only think about winning it back.
- Gambling is affecting your work, studies or relationships.

## Where to get help

- [Gambling Therapy](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org/): multilingual online counselling and support.
- [Gamblers Anonymous](https://www.gamblersanonymous.org/): a fellowship for people with gambling problems.

If you or someone close to you needs help, please don't face it alone.
