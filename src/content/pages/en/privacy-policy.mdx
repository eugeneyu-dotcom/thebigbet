This policy explains what data is collected when you use thebigbet.org, why, and what choices you have.

## What we collect

The site has no member accounts, no comments, and no forms that ask for personal details, so **we do not directly collect your name, email address or other personal information**.

As you browse, the following services may collect data automatically:

- **Hosting (Vercel)**: the server keeps ordinary access logs, such as IP address, browser type and the requested URL, in order to deliver the service and keep it secure.
- **Google Analytics**: used to understand how the site is used (for example which pages are viewed, the device used and the approximate region). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and the data is presented in anonymous or aggregated form.
- **Google AdSense**: the site may show Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads, including personalized ads based on your earlier browsing.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. The site itself does not use cookies to store your personal settings; the third-party services above (Google Analytics, Google AdSense) do. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features or ad display may be affected.

## Managing your data

- You can manage or turn off personalized ads in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- To learn how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- To stop Google Analytics from collecting data, you can install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## How we use data

Data collected is used only to improve content and the user experience, keep the site secure, and display advertising. We do not sell your data.

## Children

The site's content relates to betting information and is not suitable for minors; we do not knowingly collect data from users under 18.

## External links

Articles may link to external sites such as Wikipedia. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control and are not responsible for.
