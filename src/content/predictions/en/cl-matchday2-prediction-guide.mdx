Picking up from the [Matchday 1 prediction guide](/en/football/champions-league/predictions/cl-matchday1-prediction-guide/) (and the [Matchday 1 verification](/en/football/champions-league/verification/cl-matchday1-predictions-verified/) afterwards), schedules get a lot tighter from Matchday 2: Liverpool finish with City on Sunday and are in Linz by Wednesday; Real Madrid play Villarreal on 10/10, travel to Roma on 10/14, then head to Camp Nou on 10/25; Barcelona have Getafe, Galatasaray, Betis, PSG and the Clásico within 12 days. So in this stretch it isn't only about results and starting-XI strength — the depth of the bench and how well squads rotate matter too.

## Arsenal vs Lille: The Odds Are One-Sided, and Last Round's Upset Doesn't Close the Squad Gap

Odds: Arsenal 1.22 / Draw 6.5 / Lille 13 (10/13 19:00 UTC, Emirates)

**Form**: Arsenal, last season's Premier League champions, are second in the league on four wins and one defeat and opened their Champions League campaign with a 1-0 win over Napoli. They did suffer a surprise 0-3 loss to Brighton last round (previewed in the [Matchweek 6 prediction guide](/en/football/leagues/predictions/epl-matchweek-6-six-clubs-prediction-guide/)), but their quality is still not to be underestimated. I haven't studied Ligue 1 in depth, but outside PSG, French sides generally aren't a match for Arsenal at Champions League level.

**Injuries**: [Saliba](wiki:William_Saliba) is out until November–December and [Havertz](wiki:Kai_Havertz) is out for several weeks; [Rice](wiki:Declan_Rice) is expected to be available, while [White](wiki:Ben_White_(footballer)), [Hincapié](wiki:Piero_Hincapié) and [Mosquera](wiki:Cristhian_Mosquera) are on the verge of returning.

**Schedule density**: Arsenal have a Premier League game on 10/10, so they get less than four days' rest before this one — but Lille's schedule is no kinder, and they actually get even less rest than Arsenal.

**What the odds say**: The odds heavily favour Arsenal, almost one-sidedly. Judged by squads, that's entirely reasonable: Lille have names like [Giroud](wiki:Olivier_Giroud) and [Ayase Ueda](wiki:Ayase_Ueda), but there's still a big gap in star power compared with Arsenal. Add Arsenal playing at home and their chances of winning look very high.

**Verdict**: Arsenal lost unexpectedly last round, but facing a French side puts them under relatively little pressure here. Lille have a decent front line and defence, but in an era that rewards midfield strength, their midfield — for all its bright spots — can't compare with Arsenal's. With schedule, fatigue and home advantage all not in their favour either, I back Arsenal to win, and the better-priced handicap line is worth considering too.

## Atlético Madrid vs Manchester United: With Form This Far Apart, I'm Backing the Side With Momentum

Odds: Atlético 2.25 / Draw 4.0 / Man Utd 2.80 (10/13 19:00 UTC)

**Form**: Atlético lost 1-2 away to Liverpool in their opener; Man Utd beat Sabah 4-0 at home first time out, but have taken just five points from five Premier League games and lost to Brighton in the League Cup — a big gap between their domestic and European displays.

**Injuries**: Man Utd's [Bruno Fernandes](wiki:Bruno_Fernandes) has been playing through injury all along, [Rashford](wiki:Marcus_Rashford) and [Mainoo](wiki:Kobbie_Mainoo) are both doubts, and [Ugarte](wiki:Manuel_Ugarte_(footballer)) isn't expected back until April next year.

**Schedule density**: Roughly equal on both sides — both are playing twice in a week with their previous games close together, so both teams' physical loads will be tested considerably.

**What the odds say**: The two prices are very close, which shows the market rates this near 50-50, with Atlético's win probability slightly higher.

**Verdict**: On form alone, there's not much to say: Atlético recently beat Real Madrid in the city derby, so morale is at a high, while United have gone three games without a win apart from beating a much weaker Sabah in Europe. Going purely on form, Atlético is the obvious side, and the price probably factors in a bit of squad star power and the gap in league strength. But I personally think United's form is genuinely bad — not ordinarily bad — so I favour the higher-morale Atlético to win.

## Galatasaray vs Barcelona: However Tight the Schedule, Don't Underestimate the Firepower — I Back Barcelona to Win Easily

Odds: Barcelona 1.33 / Draw 5.5 / Galatasaray 8 (10/13 19:00 UTC, Rams Park)

**Form**: Barcelona opened with a 5-1 win over Feyenoord ([Raphinha](wiki:Raphinha) scored twice) and lead La Liga by six points; Galatasaray are last season's Turkish champions and have won four of their last five (scoring 15, conceding 4), but the intensity of the Turkish league can't be compared with La Liga's.

**Injuries**: Raphinha's thigh check came back clear.

**Schedule density**: 10/10 Getafe → 10/13 Galatasaray → 10/17 Betis → 10/20 PSG → 10/25 Clásico. Barcelona's upcoming schedule is extremely packed, and although their bench depth is sufficient, whether Flick dares to rotate is the biggest variable.

**Specific tactical weakness**: Echoing the point in my [analysis of Barcelona's no-striker attack](/en/football/leagues/analysis/barcelona-no-striker-attack/), Barcelona — lacking a physical edge — can get stuck against a deep, packed defence, but Turkish-league technical quality is lacking, so even if Galatasaray park the bus they should struggle to hold out for 90 minutes against sustained pressure.

**What the odds say**: The market overwhelmingly favours Barcelona, and given their firepower and form at the start of the season, that's a reasonable price.

**Verdict**: Barcelona are away and, as noted above, the no-striker system has its weakness, but with how hot they've been lately I don't think a Turkish side can threaten them. I back Barcelona to win easily.

## Viking vs Bayern: A 1.08 Win Price Has No Value — If You Want Returns, Look at Goals

Odds: Bayern 1.08 / Draw 11 / Viking 23 (10/13 19:00 UTC, Stavanger)

**Form**: Bayern have won four and drawn one of their last five (the only non-win being a 0-0 with Schalke), scoring 13 and conceding 2, and opened the Champions League with a 5-0 win over Bodø/Glimt. Viking only reached the league phase by beating Dinamo Zagreb 5-3 on aggregate in the play-offs.

**Schedule density**: After away at Augsburg on 10/10 they head to Norway on 10/13, and Kompany may rotate a batch of first-team players.

**What the odds say**: 1.08 practically announces the result — the straight win has no betting value.

**Verdict**: Given Bayern's form and how hot [Olise](wiki:Michael_Olise) has been lately, I can't see how Bayern lose. The 1.08 price shows the market feels the same way, and a game Bayern are all but guaranteed to win offers little profit — if you really want to bet, go for the over on goals instead, which could pay slightly better.

## LASK vs Liverpool: One of the Softest Draws This Season — Even a Rotated Bench Is a Class Above

Odds: LASK 13 / Draw 7 / Liverpool 1.20 (10/14 16:45 UTC, Linz)

**Form**: Liverpool beat Atlético 2-1 in their opener and are unbeaten in the league with three straight clean sheets.

**Schedule density**: They face City at Anfield on 10/11 (previewed in the [Matchweek 6 prediction guide](/en/football/leagues/predictions/epl-matchweek-6-six-clubs-prediction-guide/)) and fly to Linz three days later, so stamina will be a test.

**Injuries**: [Bradley](wiki:Conor_Bradley) is out long term, and [Isak](wiki:Alexander_Isak) has a minor knock and may simply be rested.

**Gap in class**: LASK can fairly be called one of the softest draws in this season's Champions League; although Liverpool have back-to-back games and their form is hardly at its best, even on the strength of their bench there is still a class gap to LASK.

**Verdict**: This is much like the Bayern game — one where it's hard to see how they lose, and even with Liverpool's bench playing, the advantage is still Liverpool's. So the advice is the same as for Bayern: the straight win may be as good as safe, but there's nothing to earn from it, so you may as well try the over on goals.

## Roma vs Real Madrid: Madrid Have a Pile of Problems, and I Lean Toward Roma's Discipline

Odds: Roma 3.30 / Draw 4.0 / Real Madrid 1.85 (10/14 19:00 UTC, Olimpico)

**Form**: Real Madrid beat Inter 2-1 in their opener; Roma only drew 1-1 with Fenerbahçe in theirs, but they top Serie A with a frightening goal difference of +11 — far from a weak side to be taken lightly.

**Injuries**: ⚠️ [Mbappé](wiki:Kylian_Mbappé) (to be confirmed before the game), [Valverde](wiki:Federico_Valverde), [Konaté](wiki:Ibrahima_Konaté), [Rodrygo](wiki:Rodrygo), [Militão](wiki:Éder_Militão) and [Mendy](wiki:Ferland_Mendy) have all been on the injury list at some point, Asencio has just had surgery for a stress fracture, and [Rüdiger](wiki:Antonio_Rüdiger) is one of the few experienced centre-backs available.

**Schedule density**: 10/10 Villarreal → 10/14 Roma → 10/18 Sevilla → Leipzig → 10/25 Clásico — fatigue and injuries stacking on top of each other.

**Home/away advantage**: The Olimpico brings real home energy, and as a Serie A side Roma's discipline is hardly going to be lacking, so they should give Madrid a lot of trouble.

**Verdict**: Madrid have again fallen into problems with possession and space (the structural issues are covered in my [analysis of Real Madrid's 2026-27 summer window](/en/football/leagues/trends/real-madrid-2026-27-summer-window/)), and the defence keeps cracking. One of their key men, [Vinícius Júnior](wiki:Vinícius_Júnior), is in the biggest slump of his career, has completely lost his old dominance down the flank, and has started having defensive problems too. The odds favour Madrid, but given how poorly they're doing right now I actually think Roma's discipline makes them the side more likely to win.

## Manchester City vs PSG: Both Convince, but I'm Betting on Champions League Mode

Odds: City 2.15 / Draw 4.2 / PSG 2.88 (10/14 19:00 UTC, Etihad)

**Form**: City are unbeaten in seven under Maresca and beat Porto 2-0 in their opener ([Haaland](wiki:Erling_Haaland) scored twice); PSG beat Slovan Bratislava 6-1 first time out, with new signing [Ferran Torres](wiki:Ferran_Torres) hitting a hat-trick, but their Ligue 1 form has been mediocre — currently sixth on two wins, two draws and a defeat. Still, they are in hot form having won their last two, and the foundation of two straight titles is still there; in the Champions League especially they seem to flip into boss mode and win no matter what.

**Schedule density**: The two sides' schedule density and rest days are about the same, so physical conditions are close.

**What the odds say**: 2.15 vs 2.88 isn't a big gap and slightly favours City, but that's probably down to form and home advantage — otherwise, on past head-to-heads or squads, PSG are an absolutely top-tier Champions League side.

**Verdict**: City are at home and unbeaten in seven, so form is good, but the club is currently mired in a financial-scandal row that could affect morale to some degree. PSG's Ligue 1 form is mediocre, yet the front three and the golden midfield remain strong, and in particular [Doué](wiki:Désiré_Doué) has looked much more confident since scoring for his national team. The two full-backs who were struggling with injuries early in the season look to have found their form again, and overall PSG now look very close to the complete side of their two-time-champion days. So while a City side at home is certainly strong, I personally still prefer PSG — though I expect this to be a thrilling, tightly fought game.

## Matchday Summary

Bayern, Barcelona and Liverpool all face opponents a clear step below them, and although all three will have to deal with some physical strain, winning shouldn't be a big problem. Arsenal's Lille do have a few stars, but on paper they still fall short of Arsenal — though Arsenal's recent fatigue and slipping form is a concern. Real Madrid are in poor form and dealing with injuries, so they could have victory stolen by Roma. The two closest games this round are Atlético vs Man Utd and City vs PSG: on the Atlético game I agree with the market and don't back a United side in poor form, but my view on City vs PSG is bolder — I think PSG could take a win with their strong squad while City are caught up in the financial storm.

## What's Hardest to Call This Round: The Games Swung by Schedule and Injuries

Schedule density is the angle I most wanted to stress this round, and also the hardest to quantify: whether Flick dares to rotate against Galatasaray and how many first-teamers Kompany changes after Augsburg won't be known until the line-ups are announced, and the Madrid game hinges on whether Mbappé can play — the variable I marked "to be confirmed before the game". Everyone weights form, squad depth and morale differently, and my view is just one of them. As always, all of the above predictions are for reference only; please bet responsibly.
