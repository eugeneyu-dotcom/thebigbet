## The Limits of This Round's Method: Line-Ups and Fitness Are the Hardest Things to Pin Down Before a Game

The biggest worry about this round's predictions is the same as it was in the Champions League: as the schedule goes on, teams start running into injuries and key players who have to be rested. Some injury lists aren't updated in real time, and it's hard to know before a game which players might be rested, so this uncertainty over the starting line-up is the biggest risk to any prediction. Then there's fitness: Barcelona's head coach has publicly said before that too many games are making it hard for players to cope physically, and in recent games some players have indeed started to drop off from playing continuously. At that point, the lower-table teams who don't have to play on several fronts have more of an advantage, which is also something to weigh, so betting carries risk, please bet responsibly.
