## Manchester United vs Roma: Nine Games Without a Win, Can Roma Pick Up the Pieces?

**Kick-off**: 11/4 (Wed) 04:00 Taiwan time / 11/3 20:00 UK local, Old Trafford. **Odds** (fetched 11/3, average of 39 bookmakers): United 2.15 / draw 3.98 / Roma 2.94 (44% / 24% / 32% after removing the margin); main goal line 3.5 (over 2.26 / under 1.61).

**Form**: United have just 3 points from three Champions League games (4-0 Sabah, 0-2 Atlético, 1-4 Como) and are now nine games without a win in all competitions; the 2-0 defeat at Chelsea last weekend leaves them on 7 points, 17th in the Premier League. Roma have 4 points (1-1 Fenerbahçe, 3-4 Real Madrid, 4-0 Slovan).

**Schedule**: United played at Chelsea on Saturday 10/31 and host Roma on 11/3 with under three days' rest; they're already out of the League Cup, so there's no extra midweek burden.

**Injuries**: For United, [Rashford](wiki:Marcus_Rashford) (groin, expected back 11/7), [Shaw](wiki:Luke_Shaw) (11/22), [Dorgu](wiki:Patrick_Dorgu) (11/22) and [Šeško](wiki:Benjamin_Šeško) (calf, January) are out; for Roma, first-choice goalkeeper [Svilar](wiki:Mile_Svilar) (foot, 11/21) is out and [Dybala](wiki:Paulo_Dybala) (11/8) won't make it either. **⚠️ This comes from a single injury page, so go by the official team news.**

**Home/away**: United beat Sabah 4-0 at home in the Champions League but lost 4-1 at Como; Old Trafford is hardly a fortress for them right now.

**What the odds say**: The market has United as 44% small favourites, more than last week's 41%; the draw is 24% and Roma 32%, so the price doesn't seem to have factored in United's slump.

**Verdict**: I have to be honest that I don't know Roma's squad or style well, but judging purely by United's recent results, nine games without a win in all competitions already says a lot, so I'm backing **Roma to win**. As for a fallback, United's firepower is a problem so I won't take both teams to score or the over, but I'm not confident enough in the under either, so I'm not taking a fallback on this one.

## Barcelona vs Aston Villa: Can Villa Hit the Back Line the Same Way Again?

**Kick-off**: 11/4 (Wed) 04:00 Taiwan time / 11/3 21:00 Spain local, Camp Nou. **Odds** (fetched 11/3, average of 39 bookmakers): Barcelona 1.26 / draw 6.82 / Villa 8.66 (75% / 14% / 11% after removing the margin); main goal line **4.5** (over 2.09 / under 1.73).

**Form**: Barcelona lost to PSG (4-2) and Real Madrid (2-1, see the [El Clásico prediction verification](/en/football/leagues/verification/el-clasico-barcelona-real-madrid-2026-10-25-predictions-verified/)) back to back, but won 6-1 against Alavés on Saturday with 77% possession; Villa have won all three Champions League games (12 scored, 3 conceded, 6-0 against Fenerbahçe) and recently beat the then-unbeaten Manchester City 2-1 in the league.

**Injuries**: Barcelona are without Olmo, [Koundé](wiki:Jules_Koundé) (ankle), [García](wiki:Eric_Garcia_(footballer,_born_2001)) (muscle), [Christensen](wiki:Andreas_Christensen) (thigh) and Casado; against Alavés, Cancelo and Fariñas covered the flanks. **⚠️ The injury page lists [Rodri](wiki:Rodri) (second yellow) and Cubarsí (straight red) as suspended, yet both started on 10/31, so I can't confirm whether it's a Champions League ban.** Villa's only listed absentee is Ruggeri (thigh).

**Home/away**: Barcelona beat Feyenoord 5-1 at home in the Champions League; Villa's only away game so far was a 3-2 win at Brugge (the 6-0 against Fenerbahçe and 3-1 against Viking were both at home), so this is their second.

**What the odds say**: The market has Barcelona as 75% favourites and the main goal line is 4.5, so it expects a shoot-out.

**Verdict**: Villa are in remarkable form, having just ended Manchester City's unbeaten run. In that game they spotted that City's left-sided full-back was strong going forward but weak defensively and pushed too high, and kept targeting the space behind City's left, causing real problems; meanwhile their powerful midfield smothered [Cherki](wiki:Rayan_Cherki) and stopped him from controlling the ball. Barcelona, having lost back to back in the Champions League and the Clásico, are also missing their first-choice full-backs, so Villa may try to attack Barcelona's back line in the same way. But Barcelona's firepower this season is staggering and they have every right to trade blows with Villa, which is a big reason the goal line is set at 4.5. Putting it together, I think Barcelona have the edge in an open game, so I'm backing **Barcelona to win**. As for a fallback, even with the line at 4.5, I still like the **over (over 4.5 goals)**.

## Atlético Madrid vs Bayern: A Depleted Bayern, and I Go Against the Market for Atlético

**Kick-off**: 11/4 (Wed) 04:00 Taiwan time / 11/3 21:00 Spain local, Metropolitano. **Odds** (fetched 11/3, average of 39 bookmakers): Atlético 2.73 / draw 3.83 / Bayern 2.34 (35% / 25% / 41% after removing the margin); main goal line 3.5 (over 2.09 / under 1.73).

**Form**: Bayern have 5 points in the Champions League (5-0 Bodø/Glimt, 3-3 Viking, 2-2 Arsenal) and on Saturday drew 2-2 at home to Dortmund, outshooting them 23 to 12 but being pegged back in the 89th minute, the latest in a run of games where they led and didn't win; Atlético have 4 points (1-2 Liverpool, 2-0 Manchester United, 1-1 Stuttgart) and won 2-0 at Levante on Saturday despite being outshot 20 to 11.

**Injuries**: Bayern are without [Musiala](wiki:Jamal_Musiala) (thigh), [Díaz](wiki:Luis_Díaz_(footballer,_born_1997)) (muscle), Gnabry, Saibari (ankle) and [Neuer](wiki:Manuel_Neuer) (foot); against Dortmund the goalkeeper was [Urbig](wiki:Jonas_Urbig), who is reported to have suffered a suspected concussion after a collision in that game, and head coach [Kompany](wiki:Vincent_Kompany) is also said to be unwell. **⚠️ The injury page also lists Kim (second yellow), Kimmich and Olise (yellow-card accumulation) as suspension risks; check the official lists.** Atlético are without Cardoso (thigh) and Koke.

**Schedule**: Both sides played on Saturday and have only three days' rest, so neither gains an edge; the difference is how complete the squads are.

**Home/away**: Atlético have played only one home game in this season's Champions League, a 2-0 win over Manchester United; away they lost 2-1 at Liverpool and drew 1-1 at Stuttgart. Bayern's away form isn't solid either (3-3 at Viking).

**What the odds say**: Bayern are still the favourites, but their margin-free probability has slipped from 48% last week to 41%, so the market has started to price in the absentees.

**Verdict**: The market slightly favours Bayern, but their injury situation is just too severe: midfield core Musiala and wingers Díaz and Gnabry are all expected to miss out, Saibari was hurt against Dortmund too, and it gets worse at the back, where Neuer is injured and even back-up keeper Urbig suffered a suspected concussion in the last game, while worst of all even Kompany has been taken ill, so this is a threadbare squad right now. Atlético, by contrast, are in good shape, "little spider" [Álvarez](wiki:Julián_Alvarez) is in decent form, and the Metropolitano is no easy place to visit. So I'm going the other way and backing **Atlético to win**. As for a fallback, although Bayern are decimated they still have [Kane](wiki:Harry_Kane) and [Olise](wiki:Michael_Olise), so I like **both teams to score**.
