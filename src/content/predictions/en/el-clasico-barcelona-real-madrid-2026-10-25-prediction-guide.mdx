## Recent Form: Barcelona Unbeaten in the League but Just Beaten in Europe, Real Madrid Scoring Four a Game

**Barcelona**: 9 wins from 9 in La Liga (39 scored, 9 conceded) and 23 straight home league wins; they lost 2-4 at PSG in the Champions League on Tuesday for their first defeat of the season ([Rodri](wiki:Rodri) rested, [Koundé](wiki:Jules_Koundé) out, [Olmo](wiki:Dani_Olmo) injured; in that game PSG's xG was about 3.7 to 2.2 and shots on target 15 to 5).

**Real Madrid**: 7 wins and 2 defeats in La Liga, 0-1 at Betis and 1-2 at Atlético away; most recently 4-1 against Sevilla and 4-1 against Leipzig in the Champions League (Vinícius started, [Diomande](wiki:Yan_Diomande) scored as a substitute); [Mbappé](wiki:Kylian_Mbappé) has 9 league goals.

## Injuries: Barcelona's Defence Is Short of Players, Real Madrid Lose Vinícius

**Barcelona**: Olmo (adductor, out about a month, injured against PSG), [Eric García](wiki:Eric_Garcia_(footballer,_born_2001)) (right thigh, 3 to 4 weeks), [Christensen](wiki:Andreas_Christensen) and [Bardghji](wiki:Roony_Bardghji) are out; Koundé (left ankle sprain) is reported differently by different sources, with some saying he's expected back soon and might play on Sunday and others saying he's out; [Raphinha](wiki:Raphinha) has just returned from a hamstring injury and is expected to start. Rodri was rested against PSG and is expected to start this one.

**Real Madrid**: Vinícius is suspended after his red card against Villarreal (reports say he is serving the second game of a four-match ban), and Asencio and [Rodrygo](wiki:Rodrygo) are out; [Mendy](wiki:Ferland_Mendy) has only partially returned to training after a long injury, and [Rüdiger](wiki:Antonio_Rüdiger) may also miss out. [Mourinho](wiki:José_Mourinho) has to rework the left-sided attack this week.

Camp Nou has seen 23 straight league home wins; Real Madrid have lost two league games away this year, 0-1 at Betis and 1-2 at Atlético, and haven't kept an away clean sheet. Home advantage is large for Barcelona.

## Verdict: Barcelona to Win, With Both Teams to Score as the Fallback

On recent form, there's no reason not to pick **Barcelona to win**. Their firepower this season has been astonishing, and although they just lost to PSG, that was without Rodri, and PSG have always been strong in Europe. Back in La Liga, Barcelona's firepower is the best around, and the rotations of the [striker-less attack](/en/football/leagues/analysis/barcelona-no-striker-attack/) leave every defence unable to cope. Raphinha and Yamal have both been excellent this season, and the other winger [Gordon](wiki:Anthony_Gordon_(footballer)) has scored fewer goals but is outstanding for his unselfish play and ability to tear a defence apart; they are without Olmo, but the Barcelona bench still has [Fermín López](wiki:Fermín_López) to cover for that. On top of that, the game is at home for Barcelona and they have one more day's rest than Madrid, so the overall advantage is clear. On the Madrid side, Vinícius is out, but I think that's actually a plus for them: Diomande's recent form is far better than Vinícius', and with Barcelona's back line missing Koundé and García, if Diomande can use his dribbling to blow open the flank, Madrid may still have a chance. But in an era that prizes midfield control, Barcelona's midfield and counter-pressing are far better than Madrid's, so backing Barcelona is absolutely the most reasonable judgement right now. For the fallback, both teams are in good form, and even 4.5 goals has a fair chance, so my first choice is both teams to score, then the over.
